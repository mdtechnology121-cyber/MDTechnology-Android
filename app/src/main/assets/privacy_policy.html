Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Data Collection

MD Technology ("we," "our," or "us") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely on your device and does not communicate with any server except:

  • Instagram's public GraphQL API (to fetch video URLs you explicitly request)
  • The content delivery network hosting the video file (to download it)

What We Access

When you paste an Instagram URL and tap Download, the app sends that URL's shortcode to Instagram's API to retrieve the video URL. No login credentials, cookies, or personal identifiers are sent. The video is saved locally to your device's Movies/VideoDownloader folder.

No Account, No Tracking

We do not require accounts, do not use analytics SDKs, do not serve ads, and do not track your behavior. No data leaves your device except the single API request described above.

Third-Party Services

The app uses open-source libraries (OkHttp, AndroidX, Material Components) which operate locally and do not collect data. Instagram's API is subject to its own privacy policy.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Since we collect no data, there is nothing to access, delete, or port. If you have concerns, contact us at the email below.
